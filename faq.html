Frequently asked questions

How long does charging take?

A home wall charger fills the battery overnight. At a fast charger you can add about 300 km in 15 minutes.

What does the battery warranty cover?

Eight years or 160,000 km, guaranteeing at least 70% of the original capacity.

Can I charge at home?

Yes. A standard socket works, and our wall charger is faster. Installation is included with your order.

Can I test drive before I buy?

Yes. Book a test drive on the Contact page, or take a car home for 7 days.

Is the booking amount refundable?

Yes. The ₹11,000 booking amount is fully refundable until your car is assigned for delivery.

How much does maintenance cost?

Much less than a petrol car. There is no engine oil to change, and regenerative braking reduces brake wear.
